The short version.

I'm Arafat. I write code in Dhaka, and most of it runs in production, not in a demo folder. Next.js and TypeScript for the web, Supabase or Node behind it, Kotlin when the product lives on a phone.

The other half of my work is security. I audit stores and websites for the openings I spend the rest of my time making sure my own builds do not have. Everything below got audited by me before it shipped.

So far that means Nyktos, the encrypted messenger I founded, where even the server cannot read the messages. ClipHaus, the clipping agency I founded, where I built the whole platform under it. And arzxsh, an Android music player that mixes songs like a DJ. Off the clock: the gym and building my own security tools.

  1. Development

    Web apps where the back office actually works: logins, payments, admin panels with roles, order flows that do not fall apart halfway.

  2. Security audits

    I go through a store or site the way an attacker would, then hand over the exact findings and the fixes. Not a scanner printout.

  3. Products

    Nyktos, ClipHaus, arzxsh. My own software, live, with real people using it. Not portfolio decorations.
